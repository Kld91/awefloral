Wholesale Delivery

We deliver wholesale orders with a shipment every week.

Shipment schedule

  • Order and payment cut-off: Sunday 5 pm Riyadh time.
  • The shipment lands in Riyadh on Tuesday.
  • You receive your order on Wednesday; we confirm the time on WhatsApp.
  • Orders paid after Sunday 5 pm go into the following week's shipment.

Large quantities and events

  • Large quantities are arranged with you within two days.
  • Event orders can be delivered in batches over 4 days. Send us the days and quantities on WhatsApp.

Delivery fees

  • Inside Riyadh: free.
  • Outside Riyadh: we currently deliver inside Riyadh only; for other cities, message us on WhatsApp.

Receiving your order

  • Enter the correct address and recipient number, and make sure someone is there to receive it at the time we confirm.
  • If delivery fails because of incorrect details or no answer, we contact you to reschedule.
  • Check and photograph the order on receipt. Details in the Wholesale quality and refund policy.

If the shipment is late

If the shipment is delayed or does not arrive for any reason, we refund you in full or deliver your order with the next shipment, whichever you choose.
