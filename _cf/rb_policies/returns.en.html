Wholesale Quality and Refunds

Cut flowers are a natural, perishable product that we order from the supplier for your order. That is why we rely on checking them at the moment of delivery.

Check your order on receipt

  • Open the boxes or bunches when you receive them, and check the varieties and quantities against your order.
  • Photograph the flowers in their packaging, any problem clearly, and the box label if there is one.
  • Send the photos on WhatsApp to 0539804321 with your order number.

Reporting window

Let us know within 6 hours of receipt. After that we cannot judge the condition the flowers arrived in.

What is covered

  • Flowers that arrive broken, wilted or damaged.
  • A variety, colour or count different from your order.
  • A quantity short of what the order states.

What is not covered

  • Slight differences in colour, length or how open the blooms are, which are natural with the season and shipment.
  • Flowers that arrive in good condition and match the order, because they are a fresh product ordered for you from the supplier.
  • Damage after receipt caused by storage, water or heat.

The solution

After reviewing the photos, we refund the full value of the damaged or missing stems, or give you a substitute you approve.

All at no cost to you.

Unavailable varieties and late shipments

  • If a variety in your order is unavailable, we refund its value in full or give you a substitute you approve.
  • If the shipment is delayed or does not arrive, we refund you in full or deliver your order with the next shipment, whichever you choose.

Cancellation

A paid order cannot be cancelled by the customer, because we place it with the supplier right after payment.

How refunds are paid

  • To the same payment method within 7 days; when it shows up may vary by bank or payment method.
  • If you paid with Tamara, the refund goes through Tamara and your instalments are adjusted there.
  • If you paid from your shop account, the amount goes back to the account balance.
  • If you took a VAT invoice in your company's name, we issue a credit note for the refunded amount.
