Privacy policy

This page explains what data we receive from wholesale orders on www.rawnbatat.com, and how we use and protect it.

Order details

When you order, we receive your name or your business name, mobile number, delivery address, and the items and quantities. If you ask for an invoice in your company's name, we also receive the company name, commercial registration and VAT numbers. We use these to prepare and deliver the order, issue the invoice and contact you about it, and we keep a record of the order for follow-up.

The order reaches the team that prepares it and the driver who delivers it, and we issue the invoice from an approved e-invoicing system. The site and order data are hosted with Cloudflare and order details reach the preparation team through a messaging app; the servers of these services may be outside Saudi Arabia. We keep order data as long as we need it to follow up the order and for invoices and legal requirements.

Shop account

If you apply for a shop account, we receive the business name and type, district, commercial registration and VAT numbers, and the contact name, mobile and email, and use them to verify the business, open the account and contact you. Signing in goes through Google Firebase, and your password never passes through us. We keep the account balance, its entries and orders, and if credit is switched on, the number and amount of the promissory note on Nafith.

WhatsApp

If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own policy also applies to that chat.

Analytics and ads

We use Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta (Instagram) pixel to count visits and orders and see where they came from. These tools use cookies. We record page and item views in our own system without a name or number. When you pay for an order, we send Meta your phone number (hashed), your first name, your IP address and browser type so we know which ad brought the order, and we keep the IP address and browser type with the order to protect against fraud.

Online payment

Payments by card, mada and Apple Pay are processed on the licensed Tap Payments page. You enter your card details directly with them; they don't pass through us and we don't store them. We only receive the payment status, amount and transaction number from them.

Paying with Tamara

If you choose to split the amount with Tamara, we send Tamara your name, mobile number, delivery address, the order items and amount, and your email if you entered one, so they can verify you and schedule your payments. Payment and your card details are handled on Tamara's page directly; we only receive the order status and amount from them. Your dealings with Tamara are subject to their terms and privacy policy.

Cart

Your cart is kept only in your browser and is sent to us only when you place the order.

Deleting your data

Message us on WhatsApp at 0539804321 and we'll delete your data from our records, except what we're legally required to keep, such as invoices.
