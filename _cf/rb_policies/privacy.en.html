Privacy policy

Raw Alnabatat Trading Co. is committed to protecting its customers' personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. This policy sets out the data we collect through www.rawnbatat.com, why we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

Data we collect

  • Order data: your name or business name, mobile number, delivery address, and the items and quantities ordered.
  • Billing data: business name, commercial registration and VAT numbers, when you request a tax invoice in your business's name.
  • Business account data: business name, activity and location, commercial registration and VAT numbers, the contact person's name, mobile and email, and the account balance and transactions.
  • Usage data: pages and items viewed, browser type and IP address.

How we use it

  • To fulfil and deliver orders and contact you about them.
  • To issue electronic tax invoices and meet legal requirements.
  • To verify businesses when opening and managing business accounts.
  • To measure site and advertising performance and to prevent fraud.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • Payment providers: Tap Payments for cards, mada and Apple Pay, and Tamara for instalments. Card details are entered directly with the provider; they never pass through us and we do not store them.
  • The e-invoicing system approved by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.
  • Nafith: to register promissory notes for credit accounts.
  • Hosting and technology providers: Cloudflare for hosting the site and data, and Google Firebase for business account sign-in.
  • Analytics and advertising tools: Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta. When a payment is completed, we send Meta the mobile number in hashed form and the first name, to measure advertising effectiveness.

Transfers outside the Kingdom

Some data may be processed on service providers' servers outside Saudi Arabia, in line with the legal rules on transferring personal data.

Cookies

The site uses cookies to keep your cart in your browser and for analytics and advertising. You can control or delete them in your browser settings.

Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to fulfil and follow up orders, and for the periods required by law for accounting records and invoices.

Your rights

You may access your personal data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to its processing, unless the law requires us to keep it. To exercise these rights, contact us on WhatsApp at 0539804321.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; changes take effect when published on this page.
