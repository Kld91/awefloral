About Rawnbatat

شركة روع النباتات للتجارة (Raw Alnabatat Trading Co.) is based in Riyadh. We import cut flowers from flower markets around the world and sell them wholesale to flower shops, florists and events.

What we offer

  • Imported flowers in full bunches or boxes, priced per stem.
  • Prices include VAT, shipping and clearance, and are fixed at the moment of payment.
  • A shipment every week: order by Sunday 5 pm and receive on Wednesday.
  • Free delivery inside Riyadh, and batches over 4 days for event orders.
  • A VAT invoice in your company's name from checkout.

Payment methods

mada or cards through Tap, Tamara, or a shop account (prepaid balance, or credit for approved shops).
