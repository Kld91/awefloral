Shop account

For shops, cafes, hotels and event planners who buy wholesale flowers from us: top up a balance and order from it, and once we have worked together for a while we open a credit account for you.

Open an account for your shopAccount holders: sign in

What the shop account gives you

Prepaid balance

Top up and order from your balance

Order from Riyadh wholesale and Holland wholesale and pay from your balance in one tap.

  • Top up 3,000 SAR or more: we add 2% to your balance
  • Top up 10,000 SAR or more: we add 3%
  • Top up with mada, Apple Pay or a card, or by bank transfer
  • If you close the account we refund what you paid and did not use
Credit account

Order now, pay later

For shops that have bought from us, with a limit that fits what they buy.

  • Opens after 4 paid wholesale orders within 6 weeks
  • A statement every week, payment within 7 days
  • The limit starts from what you buy and grows as you pay on time
  • No interest and no extra fees
  • Backed by an electronic promissory note on the Nafith platform, signed by the shop owner with Nafath
Instalments

Split the order with Tamara

Split your order into 4 interest-free payments at checkout; approval is up to Tamara.

Company prices

A lower price on every item

When you order signed in to your account, you see and pay the company price: off wholesale and Holland items.

Quantity discount

A discount on large orders

Large Holland orders get a quantity discount, shown in the cart, on top of the balance bonus.

How the account is opened

  1. Fill in the form below
  2. We contact you on WhatsApp and check the commercial registration
  3. We open the account on your email, and you sign in on this page
  4. You top up and order from your balance; credit is switched on once you meet its terms

Shop account terms

  • The account is for registered businesses and opens after we check the commercial registration.
  • The prepaid balance is for buying from Awe Floral only; it cannot be turned into cash or moved to another account.
  • The bonus is added on top-ups and is not refundable. If you close the account we refund the amount you paid and did not use.
  • The credit account runs within the agreed limit and term, with no interest and no late fees.
  • Credit is backed by an electronic promissory note on the Nafith platform for the limit, and we only ever claim the amount actually owed on the statement. Once the account is closed and settled, the note is cancelled.
  • If a payment is late, buying on credit stops; after 7 days late the account is on hold until it is paid.
  • Every order has a tax invoice, and your statement is on this page once you sign in.
  • We contact you on the mobile number and email registered on the account.
