Cancellation and changes

An order may be cancelled or changed before preparation begins, from the order page or by contacting us on WhatsApp, with a full refund. Instead, the delivery time or address within Riyadh can be changed.

Weekly subscription

A subscription includes five vases delivered weekly. Any week can be postponed for as long as needed, and the address changed to any address in Riyadh, from the order page or by contacting us on WhatsApp before the delivery time. We contact the customer before the driver sets out, and if handover fails we arrange redelivery. With each new vase we collect the previous week's vase. A subscription can be cancelled before the first delivery for a full refund; after it starts, the balance is refunded after deducting the vases received at their regular price, a quarter of the subscription price per vase, since the fifth vase is a gift with the full subscription. Instead of cancelling, the remaining weeks can be postponed or gifted to someone in Riyadh. Subscriptions do not renew automatically.
