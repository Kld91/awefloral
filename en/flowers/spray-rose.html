Same meanings as the rose

Spray roses carry 3 to 10 small blooms on one stem, so each stem looks like a mini bouquet. They add fullness and softness at a lower price than large roses.

Children: Not poisonous if a child tastes it; just mind the thorns.

MeaningSame meanings as the roseRed for love, white for purity, pink for gratitude, yellow for friendship.

How to care for it

  1. Snip off blooms as they fade so the rest of the stem stays fresh.
  2. Strip leaves below the waterline but leave some foliage above.
  3. Keep away from fruit; ethylene makes florets drop.
Did you know? One stem carries blooms at different stages of opening, so it keeps renewing itself in the vase.
