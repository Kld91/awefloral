Eucalyptus has silvery-green leaves with a fresh scent and is the most used greenery in our bouquets. It lasts more than 20 days and dries well.

Children: Chewing the leaves can upset the stomach, and they may irritate skin.

Pets: Causes drooling, vomiting and lethargy in pets if eaten.

How to care for it

  1. Re-cut and stand in lukewarm water with flower food; keep it well hydrated.
  2. The leaves leave a sticky, aromatic residue; wear gloves if your skin is sensitive.
  3. To dry it, hang it upside down.
Did you know? The name comes from Greek for “well covered”, after the little cap over each bud.
