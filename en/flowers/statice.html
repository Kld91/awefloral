Fond memories

Statice has small papery flowers in clear colours and needs almost no care. It dries and keeps its colour, so it can stay with you for months.

Children: Usually low risk, but it can upset the stomach if eaten.

How to care for it

  1. It needs very little: keep the water clean and topped up.
  2. Remove leaves below the waterline; they rot first.
  3. To dry it, hang it upside down for a few days; it keeps its colour.
Did you know? Also called sea lavender, it is an “everlasting” flower that dries with its colour intact.
