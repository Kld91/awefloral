Privacy policy

شركة روع النباتات للتجارة (Awe Floral), Commercial Registration 7030369826.

Order details

When you order, we receive your name and number, the delivery address, the recipient’s name and number if the order is a gift, and the card message. We use these to prepare and deliver the order and to contact you about it, and we keep a record of the order for follow-up.

WhatsApp

Your order reaches us as a WhatsApp message from you, so WhatsApp’s privacy policy also applies to it.

Analytics and ads

We use Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta (Instagram) pixel to see how many visits and orders we get and where they come from. These tools use cookies. We also log page and product visits in our own system, without any name or number.

Online payment

Payments by card, mada and Apple Pay are processed on the licensed Tap Payments page. You enter your card details directly with them; they don’t pass through us and we don’t store them. We only receive the payment status, amount and transaction number from them.

Paying with Tamara

If you choose to split the amount with Tamara, we send Tamara your name, mobile number, delivery address, the order items and amount, and your email if you entered one, so they can verify you and schedule your payments. Payment and your card details are handled on Tamara’s page directly; we only receive the order status and amount from them. Your dealings with Tamara are subject to their terms and privacy policy.

Cart and My orders

Your cart is kept only in your browser. The My orders page keeps your past orders, and the recipient’s name, number and address, only in your device’s browser so you can repeat an order easily. None of it is sent to us, and you can delete it with the Clear history button on that page.

Account (optional)

If you create an account on My orders, we keep your email and orders with Google Firebase in its Dammam data centre in Saudi Arabia, so you can see them on any device. Your password is handled by Google and never passes through us. You can delete your account and its orders yourself with the Delete my account button on that page.

Deleting your data

Message us on WhatsApp at 0539804321 and we’ll delete your data from our records, except what we’re legally required to keep, such as invoices.
