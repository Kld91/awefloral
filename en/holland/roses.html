About the flower

Vase life7 to 10 days

DryingDries well

How to arrange it

Stand them alone in a tall vase, or make them the main flower in a bouquet with baby roses and eucalyptus around them.

Make it last longer

  • Carefully remove any thorns below the water line, without cutting into the stem.
  • If a head droops early, recut the stem and stand it in deep water for two hours. It will straighten up again.

Frequently asked questions

When will my order arrive?

The Holland shipment lands in Riyadh every Tuesday. Orders in by Sunday 5 pm make that Tuesday shipment, and we deliver them Tuesday or Wednesday and confirm the time on WhatsApp. Large quantities are arranged with you within two days.

What does the price include?

The stem price includes shipping from the Netherlands, customs clearance and VAT. Dutch prices change every day, and you pay the price shown at the moment you pay.

Why full bunches only?

We order from Dutch farms and auctions in the quantities they sell in, and those orders can’t be cancelled. That’s why we sell full bunches.

What if an item is not available?

Your price is fixed when you pay. If an item in your order is not available in the Netherlands, we refund it in full or offer a replacement you approve.
