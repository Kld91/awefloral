Privacy policy

Raw Alnabatat Trading Co., owner of the Awe Floral store, is committed to protecting its customers' personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

This policy sets out the data we collect through www.awefloral.com, why we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

Data we collect

  • Order data: the customer's name and mobile number, the delivery address, the recipient's name and mobile number for gift orders, and the gift card message.
  • Custom card design: if you upload your own card design, it is kept with the order and used only to print it.
  • Occasion reminders: if you ask to be reminded of an occasion next year, we keep the date and your mobile number to send the reminder.
  • Account (optional): your email and past orders, if you create an account on the My orders page.
  • Reviews: the rating and comment linked to an order.
  • Usage data: pages and products viewed, browser type and IP address.

How we use it

  • To prepare and deliver orders and contact the customer and recipient about them.
  • To issue electronic tax invoices and meet legal requirements.
  • To send occasion reminders to those who request them.
  • To publish reviews after checking them, without the phone number, address or order number; the first name and family-name initial appear only with the reviewer's consent.
  • To measure site and advertising performance and to prevent fraud.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • Payment providers: Tap Payments for cards, mada and Apple Pay, and Tamara for instalments. Card details are entered directly with the provider; they never pass through us and we do not store them.
  • Delivery companies: as needed to hand over the order.
  • The e-invoicing system approved by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.
  • Hosting and technology providers: Cloudflare for hosting the site and data, and Google Firebase for accounts, whose data is kept in its Dammam data centre in Saudi Arabia.
  • Analytics and advertising tools: Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta. When a payment is completed, we send Meta the mobile number in hashed form and the first name, to measure advertising effectiveness.

Transfers outside the Kingdom

Some data may be processed on service providers' servers outside Saudi Arabia, in line with the legal rules on transferring personal data.

Cookies and browser storage

The site keeps your cart and My orders history only in your device's browser, and you can clear the history on that page. It also uses cookies for analytics and advertising, which you can control in your browser settings.

Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to fulfil and follow up orders, and for the periods required by law for accounting records and invoices.

Your rights

You may access your personal data, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to its processing, unless the law requires us to keep it. You can delete your account and its orders yourself with the Delete my account button on the My orders page, or contact us on WhatsApp at 0539804321.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; changes take effect when published on this page.
