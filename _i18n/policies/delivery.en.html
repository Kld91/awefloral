Flower delivery in Riyadh

We deliver bouquets and vases to every district of Riyadh.

  • FeesDelivery within Riyadh is free for orders of 65 SAR or more, and 10 SAR below that.
  • Express deliveryWithin an hour inside the covered area, from 9 am to 11 pm, for 20.70 SAR, free for orders of 200 SAR or more. You can check availability for your address in the cart.
  • Delivery dateThe same day or any later day, at the hour you choose.
  • Order cut-offEach hour closes two hours before it starts, and from 7 pm one hour before. For the last slot (11 pm – midnight), please order before 10 pm.
  • Time slotsHourly, from 9 am to midnight.
  • Order trackingYour order link shows its status, and on delivery day your place on the driver's route and the expected arrival time. For gifts, you can send the recipient a tracking link that shows neither the price nor the sender's name.
  • ExchangesIf an item arrives damaged or incorrect, within 6 hours of delivery. Exchange policy
  • GiftsJust enter the recipient's name and mobile number; if you don't have their address, we contact them to arrange the time.
  • Gift card14 designs, with your message printed on it.
  • PaymentOn the site when ordering, by mada, Apple Pay or credit card, or in instalments with Tamara.

How to order

  1. Choose a product and add it to the cart
  2. Choose the gift card and the delivery day and time
  3. Pay on the site by mada, Apple Pay or Tamara
  4. Track your order from the link, and see when the driver arrives on delivery day
