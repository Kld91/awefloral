Exchanges and refunds

Flowers are a natural product, prepared specifically for each order.

  • Damaged or incorrect itemsIf an item arrives damaged or different from the order, please photograph it and message us on WhatsApp at 0539804321 within 6 hours of delivery.
  • ResolutionWe replace the item with the same product or refund the full amount, at no cost to the customer.
  • RefundsRefunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 days; the time they take to appear may vary by bank or payment method. For Tamara payments, the refund is made through Tamara and the instalments are adjusted accordingly.
  • Items in good conditionItems that arrive in good condition and match the order cannot be returned, as they are natural products prepared specifically for the customer.
