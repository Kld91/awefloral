Exchanges and refunds

This policy is applied in accordance with the E-Commerce Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its Implementing Regulations.

  • Cancelling before preparationAn order may be cancelled before preparation begins, from the order page or by contacting us on WhatsApp, with a full refund.
  • After preparation beginsAn order cannot be cancelled or returned once preparation has begun, including while it is out for delivery, because flowers are a perishable natural product prepared specifically for each order. This is one of the cases excluded from the right to cancel under Article 11 of the Implementing Regulations of the E-Commerce Law. Instead, the delivery time or address within Riyadh can be changed.
  • Damaged or incorrect itemsIf an item arrives damaged or different from the order, please photograph it and message us on WhatsApp at 0539804321 within 30 minutes of delivery. We will replace it with the same product or refund the full amount, at no cost to the customer.
  • SubstitutionsIf a flower is unavailable at the time of preparation, we replace it with a similar flower in colour and style, of equal or higher value. This is not considered a difference from the order.
  • Delivery timeThe delivery time is a one-hour window, and arrival may run past it because of traffic or road conditions. We inform the customer of any expected delay, and the driver can be followed live from the order page. A delay within the delivery day itself is not grounds for cancelling an order after it has been prepared, or for a refund.
  • Missed delivery dayIf the order does not arrive on the agreed day for a reason on our side, the customer may choose a new time or a full refund.
  • Failed deliveryIf delivery fails because the recipient does not answer, the address is wrong or the order is refused, we arrange redelivery with the customer. This is not grounds for a refund.
  • Not coveredDamage caused by misuse or storage after delivery, and the natural wilting of flowers after their usual vase life.
  • RefundsRefunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 days of approval; the time they take to appear may vary by bank or payment method. For Tamara payments, the refund is made through Tamara and the instalments are adjusted accordingly.
  • SubscriptionsSubscriptions follow the cancellation terms in the Terms and conditions.
  • ComplaintsWe receive feedback and complaints on WhatsApp at 0539804321 and respond as soon as possible. Customers also have the right to file a complaint with the Ministry of Commerce on 1900 or through the Commercial Report (Balagh Tejari) app.
